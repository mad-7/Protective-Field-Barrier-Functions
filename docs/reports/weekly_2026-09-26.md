## Key figures

**The G1b boundary is decisive, not marginal.** `translate_while_rotating` final heading
error:

| speed | range over 3 invocations | limit | margin | invocations within |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.40 | 2.3585–2.3592 deg | 5.0 | **+2.641** | 3/3 |
| 0.50 | 5.0474–5.0518 deg | 5.0 | **−0.052** | **0/3** |

The spread inside the failing cell is 0.0044 deg — a hundredth of its distance to the limit.
**No cell anywhere in the grid straddles a limit**, so the straddle-FAILS rule was not
invoked. Cross-track was never close in any cell; heading is the only binding metric.
Lateral passed at every grid speed and both extensions (0.35, 0.45), so `vy_max` stays at
the top of the tested grid.

**What constrains the robot is the wheel budget, not the commanded box.** Read alone, the
clamp binding counts look like the very failure mode A6 exists to prevent — 4,059 clamped
steps. They are not:

| cell | min `clamp_scale` (the box) | min `budget_scale` (the wheels) |
|---|---|---|
| forward @ 0.50 | 0.999999 | 1.000000 |
| lateral @ 0.50 | 0.999979 | 1.000000 |
| diagonal @ 0.50 | 1.000000 | **0.829** |
| twr @ 0.40 | 1.000000 | **0.819** |
| twr @ 0.50 | 0.999996 | **0.656** |

The box removes at most two parts in 100,000 of the commanded vector. The failing cell fails
while the **wheel budget** holds it at 66% of what was asked. So the 0.50 failure is a
capability limit — the robot cannot translate at 0.5 and rotate at once inside its budget —
and not an artefact of the clamp. Reported because the binding counts alone invert this
reading.

## Surprises / deviations from plan

**1. There is a contact class no scanning safety layer can prevent.** G0's `wheel_edge` is
the only positive case the certified emulator would *not* have stopped
(`certified_would_stop_step: None`; the other four would have stopped at step 15, with
contact at step 36). This is correct behaviour, not a defect: that case's slab is **0.012 m**
high — deliberately low enough to pass under the chassis, whose underside sits at 0.0148 m —
while the scan plane is at **0.221 m**. Neither the emulator nor the PFB can see it.
**This belongs in the write-up as a stated limit of the contribution**, and it is exactly
why collisions are defined by simulator contact and never by a LiDAR threshold.

**2. G0's negative case sits inside the protective field for its whole run.** The certified
layer would have stopped `parallel_pass_gap_10mm` at **step 2**. A 10 mm pass alongside a
wall is inside any protective field, so that case establishes that the contact check does not
fire spuriously — and nothing about whether the manoeuvre is permitted. **Consequence for
D2:** if the aisle strata leave legitimate travel permanently inside the field, then stops
per 100 m measures the aisle geometry rather than the controller. Raised now, before any
policy trains on those strata.

**3. The S300 scene is dynamically inert, now established on the full grid.** The per-cell
clamp and wheel-budget binding counts are *identical* to the 2026-09-20 legacy-lidar run —
budget 2,493 / 2,664 / 2,916 in the same cells, clamp 15 steps in twr@0.50 in both. The
2026-09-23 regression could only show this for three cells.

**4. Every simulator instance refused `display_enabled=false` mid-run** — 5, 3 and 4
refusals out of 66 episode starts, on all three instances. One instance did this on
2026-09-20 and the cause is still unexplained. No verdict is affected: rendering is
trajectory-neutral, checked bit-identical, and repetitions stayed identical within each
invocation. But the run files could not say *which* episodes rendered, because display state
was recorded only per run; that is now fixed per episode. **G2 is the one gate that measures
wall clock**, so this is worth understanding before G2 runs.

**5. A stale instruction in the decision log would have broken G1b.** A 2026-09-23 note of
mine said the gate overlay `configs/gates/g1b_rerun.yaml` "only repeats base's box" and
should be retired under hard rule 15. The premise was wrong: `base.yaml` carries v_max 0.40,
the gate tests to 0.50, and the script refuses to start when the box is below the grid. The
overlay's 0.50 is a *measurement ceiling*, not a measured constant, so hard rule 15 does not
reach it. Retiring it would have broken the gate. The note is withdrawn in place rather than
deleted, and a fast test now asserts the overlay covers every preregistered speed.

## Decisions needed from supervisor

Ordered by what they block. **Q1–Q3 block freezing preregistration, which blocks training.**

**Q1. The unmapped-obstacle rates, to be preregistered before any policy trains.** You ruled
a low declared rate in train (inviting ~20–30%), a fixed rate per test stratum (0%
mapped-only, 100% unmapped-static), and that the train rate is a shared environment
hyperparameter — identical across L1, L2, L4, L5 and A1, never varied per method. I propose:

| set | rate | reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| train | **0.25** | mid-range of what you invited; one episode in four carries 1–2 unmapped obstacles |
| tune | **0.25** | identical to train, so tuning cannot select for a distribution the test sets lack |
| test `mapped_only` | **0.0** | the clean-map condition |
| test `unmapped_static` | **1.0** | every episode understates the world |

Confirm 0.25, or name the rate.

**Q2. The split seed ranges, for the record.** Proposed: train `1_000_000–1_999_999`, tune
`2_000_000–2_999_999`, test `3_000_000–3_999_999` — disjoint, a million apart, and already
asserted *geometrically* disjoint (layouts drawn from different splits share no
`layout_sha256`). Test sets to be written with the lock set, so regenerating one needs a
recorded amendment. Identity stays the triple `(seed, layout_sha256, generator_sha256)` as
you ruled, checked at load rather than trusted.

**Q3. The sensor-dropout rate.** You approved the augmentation in principle and left the rate
and mechanism until the environment landed, which it now has. I propose **per-bin dropout at
0.02 during training only**, a dropped bin presenting as *invalid* rather than as a zero
range — so the 36 validity channels vary without the policy learning to expect a degraded
sensor. Confirm 0.02, or name the rate.

**Q4. `vy_max` 0.50 against `v_max` 0.40 — please confirm the asymmetry.** Carried as "for
the supervisor to confirm" since D8. G1b measures lateral passing at every grid speed and
both extensions, so 0.50 is measured capability; the wheel budget, not the box, is the real
constraint. Squaring `vy_max` down to 0.40 would discard that capability. The policy's
normalised action scales each axis by its own ceiling.

**Q5. Should a raised-obstacle G0 case cover the sensor housing?** I waived this for the
current run rather than make an unrecorded amendment — adding a case *is* an amendment and
needs your ruling. The five committed cases cover chassis, wheels and the housing corner;
what is not covered is a raised obstacle meeting the housing above the wheels.

**Q6. Should the emulator's `warning` output come from a separate warning field?** It
currently reports the band above the one in force, which is a defensible proxy but not what a
real scanner does.

**Q7. The display-refusal cause, before G2** (Surprise 4). Harmless to every result so far;
G2 is the gate where it would matter.
